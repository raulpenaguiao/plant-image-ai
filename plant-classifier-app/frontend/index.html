Model limitations — read before interpreting results

  • Supported crops only: Apple, Corn (maize), Grape, Potato, Tomato. Anything else will still produce a prediction — it will just be wrong.
  • Leaf images only: The model was trained on close-up, isolated leaf scans (PlantVillage dataset). Field photos, fruit images, or full-plant shots are out of distribution and unreliable.
  • Class imbalance bias: The model has a known tendency to predict Corn (maize) classes. Low-confidence predictions toward Corn should be treated with extra scepticism.
  • Not production-ready: This is a portfolio prototype. The linear probe has not been hyperparameter-tuned and the CLIP backbone has not been fine-tuned on plant data.
